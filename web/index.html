A robotaxi depot in East Oakland, running

A simulation of one depot on a real parcel: vehicles leave the road to charge and be cleaned, then go back out to the riders. Change the fleet, the grid, the chargers or the rules and watch what backs up.

The plan, hour by hour

Every hour the controller draws a plan for the next 24 hours, acts on its first hour, and draws it again an hour later from where the fleet then stands. This is that plan as it stands now, over the plans that came before it.
